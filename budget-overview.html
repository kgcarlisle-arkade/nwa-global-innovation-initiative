Funding Structure — 100% + In-Kind

The WFF grant funds 100% of the program's cash costs. Arkade contributes a substantial in-kind match — dedicated workspace and Director/staff time — equal to roughly one in-kind dollar for every two grant dollars.

Budget Assumptions
  • One-year (12-month) pilot supporting a single inaugural cohort of 6–12 companies
  • WFF grant funds 100% of cash program costs ($973,000); Arkade contributes ~$521,000 in-kind
  • In-kind match equals ~35% of total program value — roughly one in-kind dollar for every two grant dollars
  • Two WFF-funded hires: Program & Education Manager and Communications Director; salaries loaded ×1.2 for benefits and bonus
  • Administration held to ~10% of total program cost
  • All grant funds administered through SEED SPOT as fiduciary; quarterly reporting against KPIs
