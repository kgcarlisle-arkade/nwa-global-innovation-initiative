Executive Summary

A proven category of philanthropic investment

02

Programs that welcome and support founders — including those from underrepresented backgrounds — and connect them into a regional innovation ecosystem are not a new idea. They are an established and growing category of philanthropic investment, present in communities across the country, including in Northwest Arkansas itself.

Foundation- and nonprofit-backed accelerators in Northwest Arkansas, Tulsa, and dozens of mid-size communities nationwide demonstrate measurable, mission-aligned results: founders welcomed and supported, businesses launched and grown, talent attracted and retained, and entrepreneurship made more accessible to people who have historically had less access to it.

Northwest Arkansas has proven demand for this model. The Walton Family Foundation's own investment in EforAll Northwest Arkansas shows what is possible when philanthropic capital backs inclusive entrepreneurship here. What the region has not yet built is a program designed for growth-stage founders — many building from outside the region — seeking to connect into Northwest Arkansas' anchor industries (consumer commerce, food and agriculture, and logistics and transportation) and put down roots. That is the gap Land + Launch NWA addresses.

The bottom line for the grant narrative: This is not an untested concept. It is a proven, mission-aligned category of philanthropic investment that delivers meaningful community impact — and one the Walton Family Foundation already supports in Northwest Arkansas. Land + Launch builds directly on that foundation, extending a welcoming, access-expanding model to the growth-stage founders who can connect most deeply with the industries that make the region distinct.

Comparable 01 · Local Proof

EforAll Northwest Arkansas

03

Location: Northwest Arkansas · Key Supporter: Walton Family Foundation · Model: Free, year-long inclusive business accelerator

Structure

EforAll (Entrepreneurship for All) Northwest Arkansas is a free, year-long accelerator that helps under-represented entrepreneurs launch and grow businesses through cohort-based programming, mentorship, and community support. It runs a Spanish-language sister program, EparaTodos, extending access to the region's Spanish-speaking founders. The program is a key part of the Walton Family Foundation's inclusive-entrepreneurship work in the region, with cumulative support that has topped $1 million.

Why It's Relevant

EforAll Northwest Arkansas is the clearest local proof that the Walton Family Foundation invests in inclusive, cohort-based entrepreneurship in the region — and that the model works here. Land + Launch NWA extends the same welcoming, access-expanding approach to a complementary segment: growth-stage founders building in the region's anchor industries, many arriving from outside Northwest Arkansas. The two efforts are complementary, not competing — EforAll supports early-stage local founders; Land + Launch connects growth-stage founders into the anchor-industry ecosystem and helps them put down roots.

Comparable 02 · Peer Place-Based Foundation

George Kaiser Family Foundation

04

Location: Tulsa, Oklahoma · Operator: George Kaiser Family Foundation (GKFF) · Model: Place-based family foundation investing in inclusive entrepreneurship and talent

Structure

The George Kaiser Family Foundation is a place-based family foundation that has made sustained, large-scale investments in welcoming people to Tulsa and supporting entrepreneurs — particularly those from underrepresented backgrounds. Its initiatives include Tulsa Remote (talent attraction), Build in Tulsa, and Avanzando Juntos, which supports Black and Latino entrepreneurs. Together they represent a long-term, foundation-led commitment to inclusive economic participation in a mid-size American region.

It is a direct parallel to the role philanthropic investment can play in Northwest Arkansas — and a demonstration that sustained commitment to welcoming and supporting diverse founders generates lasting community benefit over time.

Comparable 03 · Replicable National Model

gener8tor & gBETA

05

Location: National — 45+ communities across 25 states · Operator: gener8tor · Model: Free, non-equity accelerator backed by corporations, universities, and foundations

Structure

gener8tor's gBETA program is a free, non-equity accelerator funded by corporate, university, and foundation partners. It runs cohort-based programming across dozens of mid-size communities, deliberately expanding access to entrepreneurial support beyond the major coastal hubs. Because it takes no equity and charges founders nothing, it removes two of the most common barriers to early support.

Sector Context

Why Northwest Arkansas

06

The comparable programs above are largely sector-agnostic. Land + Launch NWA is specifically positioned at the intersection of Northwest Arkansas' anchor industries and the dense ecosystem of suppliers, talent, and partners that surrounds them.

A rare concentration of industry

Five global headquarters anchor the region's core industries — consumer commerce (Walmart, Sam's Club), food and agriculture (Tyson Foods, Simmons Foods), and logistics and transportation (J.B. Hunt) — alongside more than 1,100 CPG brand offices. For a region of just over 600,000 residents, that is a rare concentration of industry density across multiple adjacent verticals, and it shapes a founder opportunity that few mid-size regions can match.

A welcoming community, still building its connective tissue

Northwest Arkansas pairs that industry density with rapid population growth, a growing talent pool, supportive regional civic leadership, and a strong quality of life. What is still developing are the intentional channels that connect growth-stage founders — including those arriving from outside the region — into the anchor-industry ecosystem, the local talent pool, and the relationships that make a sustained presence meaningful. That is the connective tissue Land + Launch is designed to build.

Complementary to what already exists

The region's existing entrepreneurial supports — including EforAll Northwest Arkansas — serve primarily early-stage and local founders. Land + Launch NWA does not duplicate that work. It serves a distinct, complementary segment: growth-stage founders building in commerce enablement, food and agriculture, and logistics and transportation who are ready to engage the anchor-industry ecosystem and explore Northwest Arkansas as a long-term home.

Land + Launch connects a proven, inclusive, foundation-backed model to the specific industries that make Northwest Arkansas distinct — welcoming growth-stage founders into a community already invested in expanding access to opportunity.
